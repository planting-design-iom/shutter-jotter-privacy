Shutter Jotter

Privacy Policy — last updated 27 September 2026

Overview

Shutter Jotter is a photo notebook: you take photographs, mark them up, and organise them into folders and dates. Everything it does happens on your own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data we collect

None. Shutter Jotter does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. We operate no servers, the app has no account to sign in to, and nothing you photograph, write, or mark is ever sent to us.

Your photographs

Photographs you take in the app are saved to your device's own picture storage, in a folder named Shutter Jotter, exactly as your normal camera saves its photos. They are yours and they stay on your device. If you have your phone's gallery set to back up to your own Google account, your Shutter Jotter photographs are included in that just as any other photograph would be — that is a setting in Google Photos, not something this app controls or can see.

Your mark-ups — circles, arrows, notes and so on — are stored separately from the photograph itself, so the original picture is never altered. When you share a marked-up photo or export a PDF, the app makes a flattened copy at that moment; those copies are kept in the app's own private storage, where no other app and no gallery can reach them, and they are removed when you delete the photo.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps the following on your device only. It is never sent to us.

  • Your notebook — folders, dates, calendar entries, favourites, and the mark-ups you have drawn, held in the app's own database.
  • Your settings — a small number of preferences, including whether you have unlocked the paid features and how many mark-ups you have made.
  • Your branding — if you add a business name or logo for PDF exports, it is stored on the device and used only to put your name on the PDFs you create.
  • A crash log — if the app ever crashes, it writes the technical details of that crash to a file in its own private storage, so a fault can be diagnosed. Nothing is sent anywhere automatically. The file leaves your phone only if you deliberately find the hidden diagnostic screen and choose to share it.

Backup to your own Google account

Android's standard Auto Backup feature is switched on for this app. That means your notebook — folders, dates, favourites and mark-ups — is backed up to your own Google Drive, in the private area Android reserves for app backups, so that it can come back if you set up a new phone. This is Android doing it, not us: we have no access to that backup and cannot read it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings. Your photographs themselves are not part of it.

Location

Shutter Jotter can fill in a folder's location for you when you tap “Use current location”. Your device's location is read at that moment only, turned into an address, and shown to you as text you can edit or delete. The app does not record your coordinates, does not track your location at any other time, and never sends your location or your photos to us — we operate no servers and collect no analytics. The address you keep is stored only on your own device, as part of that folder, and is removed when you clear it or delete the folder.

Permissions

  • Camera — used to take photographs in the app, and nothing else. It is requested the first time you open the camera.
  • Location (approximate or precise) — used only when you tap “Use current location” to fill in a folder's address, as described above. It is requested at that moment and at no other time. The app does not request background location and cannot read your location when it is not open.

The app is fully usable without granting location. The app does not ask for permission to read the photographs already on your device.

Purchases

Shutter Jotter is free to use. Some features can be unlocked by a one-time purchase, which is handled entirely by Google Play. Payment is between you and Google: the app never sees your card details, name, or address, and receives only a yes-or-no answer about whether the purchase was made.

Third parties

Shutter Jotter contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services of any kind beyond Google Play's purchase handling described above.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting a photo, folder, or calendar entry in the app removes it. Uninstalling the app removes its database, its settings, and everything in its private storage. Photographs you have taken remain in your device's picture storage, as photographs from any camera app would — you can delete those through your gallery.
